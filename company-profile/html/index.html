Situations

Recognize Common Security Risks

Suspicious Email

An email asks for an urgent password reset through an unfamiliar link.

Recommended action: Do not use the link. Verify the request through an approved channel.

Unexpected Attachment

A message contains an invoice or document that was not expected.

Recommended action: Confirm the sender and context before opening the attachment.

Credential Request

Someone requests a password, verification code, or authentication token.

Recommended action: Do not share credentials or authentication codes.

Unknown USB Device

An unidentified removable device is found in the workplace.

Recommended action: Do not connect it to a company device. Follow the company's reporting procedure.

Lost Device

A company laptop or mobile device cannot be located.

Recommended action: Report the loss promptly so protective actions can be taken.

Guidelines

Everyday Security Guidelines

  • Use unique passwords for business systems.
  • Use multi-factor authentication when provided.
  • Lock the workstation before leaving it unattended.
  • Install approved software only.
  • Keep operating systems and applications updated.
  • Verify unusual requests involving payments, credentials, or sensitive information.
  • Use approved storage and collaboration services.
  • Avoid transferring business information through personal accounts.
  • Protect confidential conversations in public areas.
  • Report suspicious activity through the approved company process.
